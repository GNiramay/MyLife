      Install MyLife on Your Phone

      Install this web app to your home screen to use it full-screen and 100% offline without needing internet.

      Android (Google Chrome)

      1. Tap the three vertical dots (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome.
      2. Look for and tap "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
      3. Tap "Install" in the popup prompt.
      4. The MyLife app icon will appear on your phone's home screen. Open it to use full-screen!

      Other Browsers on Android

      Samsung Internet / Edge / Firefox: Tap the browser menu icon (⋮ or ☰) at the bottom or top right, then select "Install" or "Add page to" > "Home screen".

      iPhone (Apple Safari)

      1. Tap the Share button (the square icon with an arrow pointing up at the bottom).
      2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
      3. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner.
